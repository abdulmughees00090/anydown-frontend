Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download a YouTube video?

Copy the YouTube video URL, paste it into the AnyDown input bar, click Fetch, then choose your preferred quality — from 360p up to 4K — and hit the download button. No account needed.

Can I convert YouTube to MP3?

Yes. After pasting a YouTube link and clicking Fetch, select the Audio MP3 or M4A format option to download just the audio track — no video included.

What video qualities are available?

AnyDown shows every available quality for each video: 360p, 480p, 720p HD, 1080p Full HD, 1440p 2K and 2160p 4K. Higher resolutions are shown with an HD or 4K badge and depend on the original upload quality.

Which platforms does AnyDown support?

AnyDown supports over 1000 video sites including YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, Facebook, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Reddit, Twitch, SoundCloud, Bilibili and many more.

Is AnyDown free to use?

Yes — completely free. No registration, no account and no subscription. Paste your link, pick a quality, and download.

Do I need to install anything?

No. AnyDown runs entirely in your browser — no software, browser extensions or plugins required. A free Android app is also available for seamless YouTube downloads on mobile.

How do I download a YouTube video in 4K?

Paste the YouTube video URL, click Fetch, and select the 2160p (4K) option if it appears. 4K quality is listed only when the original video was uploaded in 4K.
